# TTEthernetForge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

Time-Triggered Ethernet (SAE AS6802) is the flagship deterministic networking standard for human space exploration (NASA Artemis Orion spacecraft), next-generation launch vehicles (ESA Ariane 6), and advanced civil/defense avionics architectures. Proprietary development toolkits and network design software (TTTech Aerospace / TTE-Tools) cost \$25,000 to \$55,000 per workstation license. TTEthernetForge Pro provides an accessible, browser-native development, simulation, and protocol compliance verification workbench for aerospace systems engineers, space vehicle flight software developers, and DO-254 / DO-178C certification teams.
